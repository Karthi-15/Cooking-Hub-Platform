How to Create a Cooking Class

  • Fill in the class name, cuisine type, and chef name.
  • Specify the location of the class.
  • Set the duration (hours) and the fee for participants.
  • List any ingredients provided to attendees.
  • Choose the appropriate skill level for your class.
  • Add any special requirements, such as dietary needs or equipment.
  • Review your inputs: required fields can't be left empty or filled with just spaces.
  • Click Submit to save your new cooking class. You’ll see a success message when done.
